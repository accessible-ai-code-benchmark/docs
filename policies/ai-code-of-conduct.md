# AI Code of Conduct

If you contribute to an Accessible AI Code benchmark project, you pledge to follow this AI Code of Conduct.

## General expectations

You are responsible for everything you contribute to Accessible AI Code Benchmark. This includes content generated using AI.

Content includes comments, issues, Pull Requests (PR), code, and documentation.

AI assisted contributions must be reviewed for accuracy, quality, licensing, and conformance with the [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md).

## Raising concerns

If you notice that AI is being used in ways that conflict with this AI Code of Conduct, we encourage you to raise it with the [Steering Committee](mailto:conduct@accessibleaicode.info?subject=AI%20Code%20of%20Conduct%20report).

## If concerns are raised about your use of AI

The AI Accessibility Benchmark Steering Committee is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this AI Code of Conduct. The Steering Committee will review reports fairly, maintain appropriate confidentiality, and determine any remedial or disciplinary actions.

We will take concerns seriously and respond in a proportionate manner. Not all conduct concerns require formal action. Many can be resolved through discussion, feedback, and clarification of expectations.

If concerns are raised about your use of AI, we will seek to understand the circumstances and discuss them with you openly and respectfully. In many cases, the first step will be informal feedback and an opportunity to reflect on and adjust the use of AI.

We expect all contributors to engage constructively with feedback and to make reasonable efforts to address concerns when they are raised.

### Formal action

Where behaviour is serious, repeated, or does not improve following informal feedback, formal action may be necessary. This may include being removed from Accessible AI Code Benchmark projects and/or banned from future participation.
