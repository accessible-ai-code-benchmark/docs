# Code of Conduct

If you contribute to an Accessible AI Code benchmark project, you pledge to follow this Code of Conduct in all Accessible AI Code Benchmark spaces and whenever you represent the Accessible AI Code Benchmark community.

## General expectations

* Be considerate and respectful in your speech and written communications
* Try collaboration before conflict
* Avoid demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing speech and behaviour 
* Be mindful of your surroundings, and of your fellow human beings
* Tell a Steering Committee member if you notice a dangerous situation, someone in distress, or violations of this code of conduct (even if they seem inconsequential)

## Unacceptable behaviours

* Dismissive or negative comments both written or spoken
* Hostile or sarcastic responses
* Repeatedly dismissing colleagues’ contributions
* Publicly undermining colleagues
* Disruptive non-verbal behaviour in meetings, such as eye-rolling, exaggerated sighing, or dismissive gestures
* Violence or threats of violence
* Encouraging violence towards any person, including suicide or self-harm 
* Offensive or hurtful comments about gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, mental health, neuro(a)typicality, physical appearance, body size, race, religion (or lack thereof), or socio-economic status 
* Unnecessary or off-topic sexual images
* Posting or threatening to post other people's personal information (known as "doxing")
* Deliberate mis-use of people's preferred pronouns 
* Inappropriate photography or recording without permission 
* Physical contact or virtual physical contact (like messages with "hug" or "kisses"), without the agreement of the person you're offering it to
* Unwelcome sexual attention, including sexualized comments and jokes, inappropriate physical contact and sexual advances. 
* Deliberate intimidation and stalking (online or offline) 
* Encouraging or supporting any of the things in this list

## Raising concerns

If you experience unacceptable behaviour, or witness someone behaving in an unacceptable way, we encourage you to raise it as soon as you feel comfortable doing so.

Depending on the circumstances, this may involve:

* Speaking directly to the individual, if you feel comfortable doing so
* Speaking to a member of the Steering Committee

To report a concern, email the [Steering Committee](mailto:conduct@accessibleaicode.info?subject=Code%20of%20Conduct%20report).

## If concerns are raised about your behaviour

The AI Accessibility Benchmark Steering Committee is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. The Steering Committee will review reports fairly, maintain appropriate confidentiality, and determine any remedial or disciplinary actions.

We will take concerns seriously and respond in a proportionate manner. Not all conduct concerns require formal action. Many can be resolved through discussion, feedback, and clarification of expectations.

If concerns are raised about your behaviour, we will seek to understand the circumstances and discuss them with you openly and respectfully. In many cases, the first step will be informal feedback and an opportunity to reflect on and adjust the behaviour.

We expect all contributors to engage constructively with feedback and to make reasonable efforts to address concerns when they are raised.

### Formal action

Where behaviour is serious, repeated, or does not improve following informal feedback, formal action may be necessary. This may include being removed from Accessible AI Code Benchmark projects and/or banned from future participation.
